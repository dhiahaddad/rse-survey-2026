    • 37  Who are you writing code for, supporting with your code or providing services to?
    • 38  Do you feel like you have a network of peers that extends beyond your close colleagues?
  • Publications
    • 39  Have you published a paper about your software? (For example in journals such as JOSS or Software-x)

    • 47  Which collaboration tools do you use for software development?
  • Job stability
    • 48  What is the minimum number of software developers that would have to suddenly disappear from your current project before it stalls due to a lack of knowledgeable people?

    • 50  On average, how many times a year do you take part in providing training?
    • 51  What training programs are you involved with?
    • 52  Do you teach or support the teaching of credit bearing modules or courses?
    • 53  In what capacity do you contribute?
    • 54  Are you granted time for personal skills acquisition or professional development? (For example, learning a new technique that is not specifically needed for a current project)
    • 55  What three skills would you like to acquire or improve to help your work as a Research Software Engineer / Research Software Developer? The skills can be technical and non-technical.
  • Funding
    • 56  Which of the following sources are used to pay for your effort as a Research Software Engineer?
    • 57  Do you have access to grants in your institutions/country for RSE type of work (ie not research grants)?

    • 59  Which operating system do you primarily use for development?
    • 60  What programming languages do you use at work? Please select all that apply.
