  • Desired: On average, how much time would you like to spend on

26.1.3 Participant allocation

NoteRescaled time allocations (*)

In the charts below, a bracketed asterisk (*) at the end of a bar marks respondents whose reported activity shares did not sum to 100%. For those individuals, each share was rescaled proportionally (multiplied by (100 / )) so the bar reaches 100% while preserving the relative split across tasks.

Each row is one respondent; bar segments show time shares per activity (rescaled to 100% where needed; see note above). Respondents are sorted by their largest single-task share in actual time (specialists at the top); the same row order is used for desired time.
